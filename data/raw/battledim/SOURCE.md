# BattLeDIM 2020 (L-Town)
- URL: https://zenodo.org/records/4017659 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.4017659)
- Downloaded: 2026-10-10
- Licence: CC-BY-4.0
- Citation: Vrachimis, S. G., Eliades, D. G., Taormina, R., Kapelan, Z., Ostfeld, A., Liu, S., Kyriakou, M., Pavlou, P., Qiu, M., Polycarpou, M. M. (2020). Battle of the Leakage Detection and Isolation Methods (BattLeDIM) dataset. Zenodo.
- Files used: 2018/2019 SCADA Pressures (33 sensors, metres), SCADA Flows (3 sensors, m3/h), Leakages (per-link leak flow, m3/h), dataset_configuration.yaml, README.txt, 2018_Fixed_Leakages_Report.txt.
- Not downloaded (size, not needed): xlsx copies, demands, levels, EPANET models.
- Format: `;` separated, decimal comma, 5-minute sampling, 2018-01-01 to 2019-12-31.
- IMPORTANT honesty note: per README the data were generated by hydraulic simulation of the "real" L-Town EPANET model (`L-TOWN_Real.inp`) with injected leaks. It is therefore `semi_synthetic` (simulated), not field measurements. Labels (leak start/end) are exact.
