# Teacher feedback and what KAMI did with it

This table goes through `docs/reference/faculty/Faculty_feedback.txt` item by item: what was asked, what was built, what was changed and why, and what was not done. It claims nothing beyond the artefacts in this repository. The feedback text of each item, by line numbers, is in `docs/teacher_scope_items.json`.

Reading guide: "Round" refers to the build rounds in `PROGRESS.md`. Data labelled `semi_synthetic` or `demo_fictional` are not field data.

## Summary

- **Built as asked, with substitutions:** the sensing-to-dashboard pipeline (T3, T5, T7, T16), the anomaly alerting (T15) and the citizen interface (T13), with Telegram instead of WhatsApp, local open speech and language models instead of Bhashini and a hosted LLM, and a virtual sensor node instead of hardware.
- **Built in a reduced form:** multilingual voice (Hindi and English only, T8), retrieval-grounded answers on fictional notices (T9), photo intake without image recognition (T10).
- **Not built:** physical sensors and a field pilot (T2, T3, T12), government data feeds for forecasting (T4), computer vision (T10, T14), demand forecasting (T6, T15) and tanker routing (T6, T11, T16). The reason in each case is the narrowed scope the feedback itself recommends, the absence of hardware or labelled data, or a rule of the project (no accounts, no keys, nothing invented).
- **Evidence:** anomaly method: `results/SUMMARY.md`; alert channel: `docs/notifications.md`; citizen interface: `docs/citizen.md` and `results/citizen/eval.md`; dashboard: `results/screens/`, `results/a11y/`; decisions and their reasons: `docs/decisions.md`.
