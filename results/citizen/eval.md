**What this is.** The citizen interface on authored questions about a fictional tanker schedule and fictional notices. It is separate from the anomaly-detection evaluation in results/SUMMARY.md and changes none of those results.

**Questions.** 40 authored test questions (20 English, 20 Hindi, 4 of the Hindi ones in Latin letters), written by the developer of the system: {'schedule': 22, 'ward_status': 2, 'faq': 11, 'none': 5}. Each part was run once on them; prompts, rules and thresholds were set on the 20 validation questions only. The correct answer is the structured lookup with the question's gold ward and day at the replay time 2018-01-07 11:00, with one planted open alert (node-3 (Ward 3): Infrastructure, High).

**Limits.** The questions are authored by one person who knows the rules of the system, so this measures the system on the intended kind of question, not on free citizen language. The schedule, wards and notices are fictional. The speech is synthetic speech from the text-to-speech model, which is cleaner than a real voice note. The numbers below are for 20 questions per language.

**Models (local).** Speech recognition `faster-whisper:large-v3-turbo`, translation `nllb-200-distilled-600M`, speech output `piper (hi: priyamvada, en: lessac)`, LLM `llama3.2:3b` through Ollama; device `cuda`.

## Reading the numbers

- "Answer correct": a schedule answer states every true time and no other time, no wrong tanker, the status of delivered and delayed tankers, and the possible-delay note when the ward has an open alert; a notice answer contains the key facts of the right notice; a status answer states what the sensors show.
- "No information": questions about an unknown ward or another topic must get the no-information reply, and answerable questions must not.
- Word error rate: word-level edit distance over the reference words, summed over the questions. The normalised form writes digits as words and ignores spelling variants that sound the same.
